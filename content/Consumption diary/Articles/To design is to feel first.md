## To design is to feel first
By Claire Taylor

"How do you know if a hook is good?" 

	"I see how it makes me feel"

at the pace of work and content being pushed to production, how and what content sticks? 

Working towards optimising feelings for those we serve and our own
- How does it feel to trust us with a big sum of money 
- What frame of mind are you in when you engage our service 
- What makes you feel in control or safe to give control to another human / AI
