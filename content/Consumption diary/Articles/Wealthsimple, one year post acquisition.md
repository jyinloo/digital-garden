## Wealthsimple, one year post acquisition
By Dennis Brotzky 

Wealthsimple is a very "serious" app. IMO apps are serious when they deal with money or crypto or trading and what not. But the branding, visual language and interactions baked into the components make it feel simple, feel manageable and friendly. if i can use it in singapore, please? i would start being more keen on finance.

The dashboard is an actual masterpiece. i was tasked with creating a similar dashboard for one of the fintech companies here in SG as part of the interview processes and (1) it was so difficult bc i lacked sufficient financial literacy (2) struggling to put such dense amount of information into a single viewport. not sure about this but would it be better to highlight the row instead of an arrow??? whenever i look at dense excel sheets i just tend to select the entire row so its highlighted and easier for me to compare.

> _A design system that would normally take a dedicated team months (tokens, primitives, docs, Figma sync, migration tooling) went from scaffold to GA in weeks, because agents built the mechanical surface area while humans made every design decision. AI didn't design Mint. It made building Mint cheap enough that designing it well was the whole job.’_
