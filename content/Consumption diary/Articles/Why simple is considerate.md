## Why simple is considerate
By Cleon Wong 

- Are numbers a hard sell? As a consumer, numbers don't really matter to me/i glance over them. Real words/testimonials would do much more 
- *Six cards was quite a lot to take in, so predictable layout made that easier*
- Trying to say too much on a marketing page. In reality people don't even read these. Compare your visuals vs words, choose one honestly 
- Screenshots or images with too many details on an already busy grid - adds clutter 
- Be intentional about what you ship and don't ship
