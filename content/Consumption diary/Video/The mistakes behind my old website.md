## The mistakes behind my old website
By Huy 

His old site was super polished and there was nothing inherently wrong with it - actually there were a lot of things right - it has won many awards, drew many eyes and earned compliments but he realised a core issue. It wasn’t speaking to his intended audience. His ICP potentially weren’t problem aware, so those _flashy (for the lack of a better word in my head)_ awards and descriptions of what he can’t do weren’t hitting.

*We put too much focus onto ourselves but not onto our clients*

At the end of the day, designers are hired because we are *problem solvers* (as much as I hate it as a buzz word). As much as it can look good and feel good if it doesn’t produce the result you want you’re not done.
