## How to take notes 
1. Themes
2. Highlights and observations
3. Questions

	Keep it short  be extremely selective, and use your own words. Be extra selective with quotes – don’t copy them to skip the step of really understanding what they mean.
	
	 In which context will I want to stumble upon it again?

## Reading Books

- _As you read_:
    - Underline
    - Write theme in one or two words
- _Two weeks later_: review and distill into digital garden
    - Cull things that are no longer important or interesting
