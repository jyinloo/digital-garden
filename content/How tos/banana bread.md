## banana bread

 - 3 bananas 
 - 2 eggs 
 - 115 g melted butter 
 - 150 g brown sugar 
 - 1 & 1/2 cup of AP / plain flour 
 - 1 tsp baking powder 
 - 1 tsp cinnamon

Mix wets and bananas. Sift in drys into mixture - one bowl. ==🔵Bake 175 degrees C for 45 mins==
